      How to Play

      CIVILIANS

      Everyone gets the exact same secret word. Give clever clues to find your allies and vote out the impostors.

      UNDERCOVER

      You receive a closely related but different word. Listen carefully, blend in, and avoid suspicion.

      MR. WHITE

      You get no word at all. Improvise based on others' clues. If voted out, you get one shot to guess the Civilians' word to win immediately!
